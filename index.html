Jonathan's Budgeting App

A private budgeting tool for one household. It is not a public service, and it has no customers or other users.

What it is

This is a personal application I built to track spending, set monthly budgets and follow net worth across the financial accounts that belong to me and, with her consent, my spouse. It is used only by the people who own those accounts.

What data it reads

The app connects to financial institutions through Plaid, with read-only access. It retrieves:

  • Account names, the last four digits of account numbers, and balances
  • Transaction history (dates, merchants, amounts)
  • Investment holdings and investment activity
  • Loan balances, such as a mortgage

The app never sees or stores bank usernames or passwords. Those are entered only on the bank's or Plaid's own sign-in screens. The app cannot move money.

How the data is used

Only to sort transactions into budget categories, show spending against a monthly budget, and chart account balances and investments over time. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, marketing or credit decisions.

Where it will be kept and how it will be protected

The app is still being built. Before any real account is connected, the following will be in place:

  • Data will be stored on a private server in my home, not on a public cloud service.
  • The storage will be encrypted, and the app will be reachable only over a private network.
  • Signing in to the app will require multi-factor authentication.
  • Connections to financial institutions and to Plaid will use encrypted (TLS) connections.

Consent, retention and deletion

I will link my own accounts myself. My spouse will link her accounts herself, and may ask for her data to be deleted at any time.

Data is kept only while an account is connected. When an account is disconnected, access is revoked with Plaid and its data is deleted within 30 days. Logs and backups are kept for no more than 90 days. The full policy is reviewed every year.
